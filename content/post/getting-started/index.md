In the summer of 2016, the time after the end of my BTech and before the start of my PhD, I was hanging out at home. I figured writing down what I learned about PhD applications might be useful for the upcoming batches of applicants. So I wrote up this document. 

The specific target audience for this document are students from the same bachelor's background as me (Chemical Engineers, international students), but I have been told that the guide has come in handy to others too. If you are considering applying for a PhD, give it a try.

Here is the link: \
[tinyurl.com/apping-guide-jyot ](https://tinyurl.com/apping-guide-jyot)

If you need guidance about a postdoc application, feel free to reach out. I may or may not write a guide in the future, about this application process which I enjoyed even more than a PhD application!
