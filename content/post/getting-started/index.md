In the summer of 2016, the time after the end of my BTech and before the start of my PhD, I was hanging out at home. I figured writing down my what I learned about PhD applications might be useful. So I wrote up this document. The target audience for this would be students from the same bachelor's background as me (Chemical Engineering), but I have been told that the guide has come in handy to others too. If you are considering applying for a PhD, give it a try: [here is the link](https://tinyurl.com/apping-guide-jyot).

If you need guidance about a postdoc application, feel free to reach out!
