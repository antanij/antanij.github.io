I serve as an Executive Editor at the *[Journal of Life Sciences](https://www.journaloflifesciences.org/index.php)*, a postdoc-led community initiative. Through our **JoLS-Pub** program, we invite scientists to write summary articles that explain their recently published technical research in language accessible to a high-school–educated audience.

I recently contributed an article to this initiative myself—it was a fun and rewarding experience. [Here is the article.](https://www.journaloflifesciences.org/archives/1561/glowing-viruses-how-strong-is-their-grip.htm)

I highly encourage other scientists to participate and help bring publicly funded research back to the public in a way that’s engaging and understandable.
