Our [2021 article](https://elifesciences.org/articles/63936) revealed that *H. pylori* flagellar motors employ a switching strategy similar to *E. coli*. Due to different placement of flagella, this strategy should not allow *H. pylori* to chemotax effectively. We discuss this conundrum in this article.
